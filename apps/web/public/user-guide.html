3. Workshop — the complete module

This is the one module built end to end. A job card moves through a state machine: each status has a defined set of legal next statuses, and nothing can write a status directly. An action that is not allowed yet is disabled with the reason shown, rather than failing later.

Draft→Checked-In→Estimate Preparation→ Approval Pending→Approved→Repair In Progress→ Repair Completed→Invoiced→Paid→Delivered

4. Inventory

Stock is held as a ledger of movements, not a number that gets edited. Every receipt, issue and adjustment is a row. The current balance is the sum of that ledger. The figure stored on the product is only a cache.

The Inventory dashboard reconciles the cached figure against the ledger every time it loads, and names any product where the two disagree. If stock is ever wrong, you can see exactly which movement caused it.

Available stock

ON HAND − RESERVED = AVAILABLE. Parts reserved for an open job card are not offered to another.

5. Finance

Receivables are derived, never stored. What a customer owes is computed from invoices and receipts on demand. A balance therefore cannot drift away from the documents behind it, and there is no figure to correct by hand.

  • Receivables — outstanding by customer, with ageing buckets
  • Transactions — every receipt, by mode
  • Dashboard — collection by payment mode, outstanding totals

6. Customers & Vehicles

  • Customers: individual or business, GSTIN, credit limit, full create and edit
  • Vehicles belong to a customer; one customer may hold many
  • Both can be added mid-flow from the job card without losing the form
  • A customer's page shows their vehicles and job card history

7. Static modules — what to expect

Important. The 44 static screens are convincing. They have real column headings, realistic sample rows and working add forms — but nothing saves. They exist to agree the shape of each module before it is built. Every one of them says so on the page.

Recognising them: the sidebar tag, a banner on the page, and a footer note on the list.

8. End-to-end flows

A. The customer lifecycle

From the first phone call to a returning regular. The badge on each step says whether it works today.

Enquiry or walk-in LIVE Someone arrives or calls. Search by mobile, name or vehicle number to see whether they already exist — a returning customer is never entered twice.
Register the customer LIVE Individual or business, mobile, email, address, GSTIN and credit limit. Can be done from the job card form without losing what is already typed.
Register their vehicle LIVE Registration number, make, model, fuel, chassis and engine number. One customer can hold many vehicles; the job card always asks which one.
First visit — a job card LIVE The vehicle's journey begins. See flow B below.
They approve, pay and collect LIVE Estimate approval, invoice, payment and gate pass are all recorded against the customer, not just the vehicle.
History builds LIVE The customer page shows every vehicle they own, every job card, and what they owe. On the next visit the advisor sees what was done last time.
Service reminder is set PARTIAL A reminder km and period are captured on the job card and stored. Nothing sends them yet — no SMS or email gateway is connected.
They return LIVE Searching their mobile brings up the customer, their vehicles and their history in one step.
Membership and loyalty PENDING Plans, members and renewals exist as screens, but a member's discount and free-service count are not yet applied to a job card.
Compliance reminders PENDING Insurance and PUC expiry are not surfaced at check-in, which is where they would be useful.

B. Vehicle in, vehicle out

Book the vehicle inJob Card → Add. Search the vehicle or register a new customer. Record km and fuel, tick accessories, note existing dents, take photos.
Complete check-inConfirms the vehicle is physically received. The card moves out of Draft.
Build the estimateAdd labour and parts. Parts search shows live availability.
Send for approvalThe card waits at Approval Pending. Items cannot be edited while the customer is deciding.
Record the decisionApproved, or rejected with a reason.
Assign and repairAssign a technician and a bay. A bay already in use cannot be picked twice.
Issue partsStock moves out of inventory and onto the job card in one step. If stock is short, the issue fails and nothing changes.
Complete the repairExtra work added during repair is marked as additional, not part of the original estimate.
InvoiceNumbered for the financial year. Totals come from the lines; nothing is retyped.
Take paymentFull or partial, by any mode. Status follows the balance automatically.
DeliverComplete the handover checklist, issue the gate pass, hand over. Delivery is blocked until the checklist is complete.

C. Parts from purchase to invoice

Product created→Opening stock→Reserved for job card→ Issued→On the invoice

Each arrow writes a ledger row. The stock ledger screen shows the full history for any product.

D. Money in

Advance at booking→Invoice raised→Receipt recorded→ Receivable reduces→Ageing updates

No step is entered twice. The receivable is a calculation, not a record.

9. Rules that govern the data

RuleWhy it matters
Money is stored in paiseWhole numbers only. Rupee decimals accumulate rounding error over thousands of lines.
Status changes go through the machineNo screen can set a status directly, so a job card cannot reach a state it has no path to.
The ledger is the truthStock is the sum of its movements. The stored number is a cache, and it is checked.
Receivables are derivedNothing to reconcile, because there is no second copy.
Issuing parts is all-or-nothingLedger, balance and job card line move together, or none of them do.
Every record carries company, branch and financial yearWritten from day one so multi-branch is never blocked later.

10. Known gaps

Specified but not built

  • Inspection — a lifecycle stage with no implementation
  • Quality Check — spec'd to gate delivery; only a handover checklist exists
  • Bay Board — bay exists as a field, not as a board
  • Rework loop — the state machine has no backward transition. Adding QC later means changing the machine, not just adding a screen. Worth deciding before data exists.

Built but not connected

  • Membership plan discount and free-service count are not applied to a job card
  • Customer Voice uses a hardcoded list instead of the Observation Library
  • The Services and Tax Rate masters are not read; custom lines assume 18%
  • Quotation does not convert to a job card
  • Compliance expiry is not surfaced at check-in
  • Custom Fields are defined but applied nowhere

Environment limits

  • No server or database — data is per-browser and lost when site data is cleared
  • Photos are held in the browser; there is no file storage
  • No SMS, WhatsApp or email is actually sent
  • No authentication, roles or audit trail beyond the job card timeline

Not yet verified by hand

  • Create Job Card compiles, serves and passes type checking, but has not been clicked through end to end with every field
